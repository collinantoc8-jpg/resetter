HOW TO USE
1. Turn off antivirus temporarily (these tools are flagged as false positives).
2. Run Adjprog.exe (the Epson Adjustment Program).
3. Click 'Select', choose your printer under Model Name, then OK. Port = Auto Selection.
4. Choose 'Particular adjustment mode' > 'Waste ink pad counter' > OK.
5. Tick 'Main pad counter', then click 'Initialization'.
6. Turn the printer off, then on again.
